Privacy Policy

Poyumi — Tiny Pocket Friends
Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Poyumi — Tiny Pocket Friends ("Poyumi", "the game") handles information. The game is provided by PIXIVO.

Game information stored on your device

Poyumi stores the pet name you choose, pet type, levels, virtual coins, quests, owned items, room and garden layouts, and game preferences locally on your device. These details are used to keep your game progress and personalize your experience. In the current release, Poyumi does not send this save information to a developer-operated server and does not require an account.

Data collection and sharing

The current game release does not include an advertising SDK, developer-run analytics, cloud saving, or multiplayer functionality. The game developer does not intentionally collect or share your locally saved pet information with third parties. The Google Play platform and your operating system may separately process installation, device, and diagnostic information under their own privacy policies.

Notifications

If your device supports it, Poyumi may ask your permission to show optional reminders to return to your buddy. You may decline or disable notification permission through your device settings. This release does not use a developer-operated remote push-notification service; reminders may not run when the app is fully closed.

Payments and purchases

Virtual coins and rewards in this version are gameplay features. Demo coin packs do not charge real money, and this release does not use Google Play Billing or request payment information.

Children's privacy

Poyumi does not intentionally collect children's personal information through developer-operated servers. The game does not have chat, online profiles, or multiplayer features in this release. Any updates introducing new data practices will require a revised privacy policy and applicable disclosures.

Security, retention, and deletion

Game progress is kept in the app's local storage on your device. No remote account is created. The information remains until you delete/reset game progress, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the game. Clearing app data or uninstalling may permanently remove game progress. Because the current game does not transmit saves to a developer server, there is no remotely stored game account to request deletion of.

Contact

For questions about privacy, contact pixivo.own@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Poyumi's features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
